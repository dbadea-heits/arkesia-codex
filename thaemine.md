# Thaemine Solo: short guide

Collected 2026-09-28. Western servers.

Thaemine Solo opens at item level 1610 and pays **1,600 / 2,000 / 2,800 gold** per gate. All of it is roster-bound. Gate 3 is by far the longest gate. If it drags on an alt, stopping after Gates 1–2 still gets you 3,600 gold.

**Before you go:** practise the Clash key presses in Trixion's training area. Bring Destruction Bombs, which Gate 1 needs, and Dark Grenades, which help in every gate.

"x145" etc. means how many HP bars the boss has left. Sidereals are the ally-summon skills.

## Gate 1: bleeding and tentacles

Items: Destruction Bomb, Dark Grenade, Atropine or Stimulant.

- **Organs:** a stagger or counter makes the boss spit organs onto the ground. Break them and pick up the blood orbs; each stack gives +10% damage, up to 10. Any you leave go back to the boss, and she gets 20% damage reduction.
- **Bleed:** at 20 stacks you're stunned for 10 seconds, so avoid hits.
- **x145 and x55, tentacles:** run to the edge of the arena without touching the blue lines. Destruction Bomb the tentacles and kill at least 6. Stay out of the yellow pull zones, then stagger her.
- **x145–100, craters:** a successful counter or stagger marks you. Stand in a crater so her attack lands on it, and hit the **same crater twice**.
- **x100, wipe check:** keep away from the craters. Destruction Bomb the tentacle with the **red orb**, then **stand in the yellow zone** so it pulls you up to her. If you didn't get the same crater hit twice, press Balthorr when Wei's text pops up. If Balthorr isn't charged it's a wipe, so don't push her to x100 early.
- **x38, eyes:** her hands turn into claws and the eyes on the floor point at the safe spot. Go there and dodge her last dash with spacebar.

## Gate 2: counters and staggers

Items: Sacred Charm, Dark Grenade, Panacea.

- **Weakness:** you get stacks from blue attacks. If lightning hits you while you have stacks, you explode. Play safe until they wear off.
- **x135:** go to the bottom of the bridge and counter the orb. Then counter the boss **only when he flashes blue**; countering a red flash is a wipe.
- **New arena:** after he raises his wings and sends out a shockwave, use **Azena**.
- **x72, wipe check:** look at the colour swirling around him. Hit the orbs of the **opposite colour** while facing him so they fly into him. Finish the stagger bar with your own skills, then use Hyper Awakening.
- **x18, three waves:** 8 weak mobs (let them bunch up in the middle), then 4 elites (Dark Grenade), then 2 dragons. **Don't counter** the dragon's counter attack. Dodge its charge up or down, get behind it, and aim **Thirain** so it hits both dragons.
- **Darkness debuff (last phase):** you have 4 seconds to reach the edge at 12 or 6 o'clock and drop the puddle there. Don't stack puddles on each other.

## Gate 3: the real fight

Items: Panacea, Dark Grenade, Time Stop or Atropine.

Every 40 seconds Thaemine does a special area attack. At some HP ranges it's the enhanced version:

| Attack | Normal version | Enhanced version |
|---|---|---|
| Cube | Upright sword, strikes to his left | Sword over right shoulder, strikes in front |
| Circle | Stand in the gap between the red | Stand behind, in front, or at the sides |
| Pizza | Stay in the safe slice through 2 explosions | Start in the safe slice, then move to where it exploded |

Enhanced happens from x255 until the first arena break, and again from x90 to the end.

Things that last the whole gate:

- Every hit adds a Mortal Wound stack: +20% damage taken for 2 minutes, and it can't be cleansed. Avoid getting hit.
- When a blue sword appears over your head, drop it at the edge.
- After a bright flash, press G on the glowing spot to start a **Clash**. A Perfect gives +10% attack for 25 seconds.

| HP | What to do |
|---|---|
| x300 | Go to the same side as the hand he raises. Wait for the ball to explode, then damage the sword |
| x275 | Spacebar through the darkness wave and remember the safe spots |
| x255 | Dodge the red pizza and hit the sword. Whoever he's targeting runs to 9 o'clock |
| x225 | Spacebar through the wave, then go to a safe spot at the bottom |
| **x210** | He's on his horse with a 40-bar shield. **Clash 1**, then **Inanna**, then break the shield. Clash 2 if it's still up. **Failing is a wipe** |
| x210–90 | He tries to break the arena every 45 seconds. Do **Clash 3**, let the next break happen, then do Clash 4 or use **Nineveh** in front of him |
| x90–55 | Fog closes in from the edges. Put all the shadow clones in one spot and push damage |
| x55 | Typing minigame with **reversed movement**. Get to a safe spot, kill your clone, then use **Wei** as soon as you can |
| x43 | **Clash 5 is behind him.** Fog now spreads from the centre at 20, 70 and 120 seconds, so finish fast |

**Wipe checks:** Gate 1 at x100, the red-flash counter and the x72 stagger in Gate 2, and the x210 shield in Gate 3.
