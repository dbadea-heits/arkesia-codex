## How it plays

- **Identity: the Divine Knight node (Enlightenment tier 1).** You can't use Holy Skills. The Piety Meter is always full, so ![Sacred Executioner](assets/icons/holyknight/sacred-executioner.png) Sacred Executioner (Z) never runs out and its +20% damage is always on, even with Z off. Turning Z on gives Divine Knight (+15% move speed) for 60 s, and X becomes ![Judgment Blade](assets/icons/holyknight/judgment-blade.png) Judgment Blade.
- **Stacks.** Each Punish Skill hit (1 s internal cooldown) gives Target Weak Point for 5 s (+8% crit damage taken), +1 ![Sword of Punishment](assets/icons/holyknight/sword-of-punishment.png) Sword of Punishment (max 30) and +3 s Divine Knight. Judgment Blade hits build ![Judgment Sword](assets/icons/holyknight/judgment-sword.png) Judgment Sword stacks (max 3). Turning Z off drops them all (3,306 damage and 2% mana per stack). Most players whose solo damage feels low aren't doing this; with 111 cores X releases them for you through Destiny.
- **Counters.** ![Executioner's Sword](assets/icons/holyknight/executioner-s-sword.png) Executioner's Sword no longer counters; counter with ![Charge](assets/icons/holyknight/charge.png) Charge (its thrust).
- **Angle:** any. 111 and pre-grid Judgment don't depend on attack angle.
- **Solo Mode buff:** pick True Supporter (KR 진정한 지원가, about +65% damage per the author; global name not confirmed). He says it makes Judgment the fastest class in Solo Mode whichever core you run.
- Main difficulty: stacking Judgment Blade and Flash Slash chains in the right order, and keeping crit near cap (this is a cooldown-reset core).

## Setup

- **Engravings:** Grudge (stone Lv.2), Raid Captain, Keen Blunt Weapon (stone Lv.3) are fixed; Adrenaline plus Cursed Doll for the rest, with Mass Increase or Stabilized Status as alternatives (all level 4 Relic books)
  - Raid Captain is fully effective at about 641 Swiftness (with Ark Passive +15% move speed, the stat-food feast and craving buffs)
  - Mass Increase is workable because the side node Prelude of Punishment gives +5% attack speed
  - Stabilized Status is sustainable thanks to the 20% shield from the side node Protective Judgement
- **Stats and Ark Passive:** crit **120%** with Blunt Thorn, **100%** with Standing Striker; short on it, put 1–2 more points into Keen Sense. The author's points are 140 / 101 / 70; at 1640 you'll have fewer, so fill in this order:
  - Evolution 140: T1 Swiftness 12, Crit 28 · T2 Limit Break 3 · T3 Zealous Smite 2 · T4 Master 1, Critical 1 · T5 Blunt Thorn 2
  - Enlightenment 101: T1 Divine Knight 1 · T2 Piety Training 3 · T3 Judgment 3, Prelude of Punishment 1 · T4 Protective Judgement 1, Blessing of Judgment 3
  - Leap 70: T1 Release Potential 4, Instant Spell 2, Unleashed Power 5 · T2 Execution Sentence 3
  - Short on crit (no Relic Adrenaline / low accessory rolls): replace Limit Break with **Keen Sense** (+4% crit) plus more Crit. With Blunt Thorn the author also suggests Keen Sense + Optimized Training instead of Limit Break
- **Gems (3 Doom + 8 Blaze):**
  - Doom: Judgment skills (Judgment Sword + Sword of Punishment + ![Judgment Blade](assets/icons/holyknight/judgment-blade.png) Judgment Blade, about 38–40% of damage) > ![Flash Slash](assets/icons/holyknight/flash-slash.png) Flash Slash (32%) >>>> ![Holy Sword](assets/icons/holyknight/holy-sword.png) Holy Sword (5%)
  - Blaze (cooldown): Judgment skills >>> Flash Slash >> Holy Sword > ![Flash Thrust](assets/icons/holyknight/flash-thrust.png) Flash Thrust, ![Spin Slash](assets/icons/holyknight/spin-slash.png) Spin Slash, ![Executioner's Sword](assets/icons/holyknight/executioner-s-sword.png) Executioner's Sword, ![Dash Slash](assets/icons/holyknight/dash-slash.png) Dash Slash, ![Execution of Justice](assets/icons/holyknight/execution-of-justice.png) Execution of Justice
  - Priority: Judgment skills Doom > Flash Slash Doom > Judgment skills Blaze >>> Flash Slash Blaze (3–4 levels lower is fine). Upgrade the three main gems; the rest can be free gems
- **Cards:** Light of Salvation 6-piece, all cards 5★: −30% Dark damage taken, +15% Holy damage, attacks become Holy

## Rotation

Main skills: ![Execution of Judgment](assets/icons/holyknight/execution-of-judgment.png) Execution of Judgment (hyper awakening), ![Flash Slash](assets/icons/holyknight/flash-slash.png) Flash Slash, ![Judgment Blade](assets/icons/holyknight/judgment-blade.png) Judgment Blade (X), ![Holy Sword](assets/icons/holyknight/holy-sword.png) Holy Sword. After each one, use your Quick Recharge skills ![Flash Thrust](assets/icons/holyknight/flash-thrust.png) Flash Thrust and ![Spin Slash](assets/icons/holyknight/spin-slash.png) Spin Slash (2 stacks); ![Dash Slash](assets/icons/holyknight/dash-slash.png) Dash Slash and ![Charge](assets/icons/holyknight/charge.png) Charge also carry Rare Quick Recharge runes.

- **Before the pull:** cast X once in the air at the repair NPC to store Destiny. Keep your Adrenaline stacks; spam skills on the loading screen to keep them.
- **Burst:** (![Execution of Judgment](assets/icons/holyknight/execution-of-judgment.png) Execution of Judgment – ![Holy Sword](assets/icons/holyknight/holy-sword.png) Holy Sword) – ![Flash Slash](assets/icons/holyknight/flash-slash.png) Flash Slash ×3 – ![Judgment Blade](assets/icons/holyknight/judgment-blade.png) Judgment Blade – ![Flash Slash](assets/icons/holyknight/flash-slash.png) Flash Slash ×4 – (![Execution of Judgment](assets/icons/holyknight/execution-of-judgment.png) Execution of Judgment – ![Holy Sword](assets/icons/holyknight/holy-sword.png) Holy Sword). Then cycle Quick Recharge: ![Flash Thrust](assets/icons/holyknight/flash-thrust.png) Flash Thrust – ![Spin Slash](assets/icons/holyknight/spin-slash.png) Spin Slash – ![Dash Slash](assets/icons/holyknight/dash-slash.png) Dash Slash – ![Flash Thrust](assets/icons/holyknight/flash-thrust.png) Flash Thrust – ![Spin Slash](assets/icons/holyknight/spin-slash.png) Spin Slash – ![Charge](assets/icons/holyknight/charge.png) Charge.
- **Before cutscenes or mechanics:** turn Sacred Executioner off by hand to release your stacks, cast X in the air while you wait, then burst again. During stagger checks, hit the boss with X instead of the air, and hold Flash Slash while other skills build Sword of Punishment stacks.
- **End of fight:** if X is on cooldown, press Z to force-release your stacks.
- **Without 111 (pre-grid or the other codes):** build Swords of Punishment with Punish Skills and Judgment Swords with X; in the damage window **turn Z off** to drop all swords; use ![Divine Justice](assets/icons/holyknight/divine-justice.png) Divine Justice (hyper awakening, same damage with Z on or off) right after turning Z off.
- X has no wind-up or recovery when chained right after another Punish Skill (turning Z on counts), so it casts faster.
- ![Execution of Justice](assets/icons/holyknight/execution-of-justice.png) Execution of Justice is the hit-immunity skill (Light Explosion tripod). ![Dash Slash](assets/icons/holyknight/dash-slash.png) Dash Slash is the better movement skill; ![Punishment](assets/icons/holyknight/punishment.png) Punishment gives a little more stagger and damage.
- Videos from the author's solo post: [Hyper Awakening timing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8r4xVOr9L7g), [Z usage / sword drop + X chaining](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VF14qWnvFbc), Kazeros [Act 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NCV9ji3bzXo), [Act 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cpisxUiG4DQ), [Act 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_dezDitRbWs).

## Ark Grid

Order cores at 14P, one of them at 17P. The pick assumes the **111** set; core names from [Maxroll's Ark Grid overview](https://maxroll.gg/lost-ark/resources/ark-grid-overview) (global names).

| Slot | Core | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Order Sun | Divine Power | Its 14P effect is the core mechanic: X triggers Destiny, which gives 3 stacks of Divine Power, letting you chain ![Flash Slash](assets/icons/holyknight/flash-slash.png) Flash Slash 3–7 times. Put your **one 17P here** (the author's 111 priority is Sun > Moon > Star, by how much damage each 17P adds) |
| Order Moon | Hour of Punishment | 14P |
| Order Star | Punishing Sword | 14P |

- Chaos cores for Judgment, in order of value: Flashy Attack > Smoldering Strike > Attack. For a DPS, Ancient upgrades on Chaos cores are worth more than on Order cores.
- Solo-only: don't move to 222. Back-attack DPS are weaker 1-on-1 against a boss, and the 222 Star core has a damage penalty ([author's 2026-08-23 reply](https://www.inven.co.kr/board/lostark/5558/88648)). Before Ark Grid (it needs 1700), don't rush the 222 core either.
