## How it plays

- The Phase Dial advances with time and skill hits. Wind-ups reduce remaining cooldowns and grant Acceleration. When the minute hand passes 12, ![Timeline Divergence](assets/icons/dimensionalist/timeline-divergence.png) Timeline Divergence becomes available.
- Timeline Divergence leaves an echo and pauses the dial and cooldowns for 15 seconds. Leave with ![Timeline Collapse](assets/icons/dimensionalist/timeline-collapse.png) Timeline Collapse, use End Split Timeline, or let the window expire. HP returns to its pre-Divergence value, so do not drink potions inside it.
- Delayed-hit skills such as ![Pinpoint Strike](assets/icons/dimensionalist/pinpoint-strike.png) Pinpoint Strike, ![Temporal Crush](assets/icons/dimensionalist/temporal-crush.png) Temporal Crush and ![Karmic Verdict](assets/icons/dimensionalist/karmic-verdict.png) Karmic Verdict wind the dial when their hits land; let them resolve before pressing Divergence.
- ![Cross Thrust](assets/icons/dimensionalist/cross-thrust.png) Cross Thrust should be fully charged. Press it twice with a half-beat gap rather than spamming it.
- No angle is needed: Hit Master is non-directional, so Time Wielder is not a back-attack build.

## Setup

- **Engravings:** Grudge, Adrenaline, Keen Blunt Weapon, Hit Master and Cursed Doll. Stabilized Status can replace Cursed Doll.
- **Stats and Ark Passive:** Specialization first with Swiftness as the other base stat. The 111 code carries Evolution Crit 10 / Specialization 30 and Time Wielder 1, Singularity 1, Acceleration Enhancement 3, On the Hour 1, Hidden Enhancement 1 and Timeline Collapse 3.
- **Gems:** For 222, prioritize Dimensional Break damage + cooldown, Timeline Divergence damage, Temporal Crush cooldown, Cross Thrust / Temporal Crush damage, then Boundary Break / Forewarning / Needle Strike cooldowns. For 111, add Pinpoint Strike damage and cooldown.

## Rotation

- Opener: ![Airsplitter](assets/icons/dimensionalist/airsplitter.png) Airsplitter → ![Forewarning](assets/icons/dimensionalist/forewarning.png) Forewarning → ![Needle Strike](assets/icons/dimensionalist/needle-strike.png) Needle Strike → ![Cross Thrust](assets/icons/dimensionalist/cross-thrust.png) Cross Thrust → ![Karmic Verdict](assets/icons/dimensionalist/karmic-verdict.png) Karmic Verdict → ![Dimensional Break](assets/icons/dimensionalist/dimensional-break.png) Dimensional Break → Divergence block.
- Divergence block: Timeline Divergence → Karmic Verdict → Dimensional Break → ![Temporal Crush](assets/icons/dimensionalist/temporal-crush.png) Temporal Crush → ![Boundary Break](assets/icons/dimensionalist/boundary-break.png) Boundary Break → Timeline Collapse.
- Repeat Forewarning → Needle Strike → Temporal Crush → Boundary Break → Airsplitter → Cross Thrust; then Forewarning → Needle Strike → Pinpoint Strike → Airsplitter; then Forewarning → Needle Strike → Cross Thrust → Dimensional Break and return to Divergence.

## Ark Grid

Target Time 222: Twisted Timeline in Order Sun, Timeline in Order Moon and Distortion in Order Star. Twisted Timeline is mandatory at Relic 17P; Timeline needs 14P minimum. Until those cores are available, play Time 111. Space cores do nothing on this Enlightenment tree.
