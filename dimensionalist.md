# Dimensionalist, Time Wielder: Solo Mode skill codes

Switching from Space Wielder to **Time Wielder** (시간 관리자). Inven's tier lists rate Time Wielder S and Space Wielder A on Extreme Mordum, the only list that rates the Dimensionalist (Avg 10.0 vs 7.0; `tierlist.md`). Time Wielder is also non-positional: no back attack, no rift to open and close, which suits Solo Mode.

**Brief:** Solo Mode (gold-funnel alt, no party, no support), region CE, item level 1704.5 → 1710 (1715 / 1720 optional, Rapid Growth selectors close 2026-12-16). Owned cores: Multislash, Precise Control, Dimensional Annihilation. Only Dimensional Annihilation is a Time core, and it is the Time 333 Sun core that almost nobody plays (see Your cores), so the character has **no usable Time cores yet**.

## The plan in two steps

| Step | Ark Grid | Code |
|---|---|---|
| **Now** | None needed: Time 111 runs without core effects | Time 111, Pinpoint Strike without a gem (Step 1) |
| **Switch when** Twisted Timeline ≥ 17P, Timeline ≥ 14P, Distortion ≥ 10P are in | Time 222 | Time 222, Dial Acceleration (Step 2) |

Why 222 over 111: 111's core effects are only small buffs and no 111 core is mandatory, so a Relic selector spent on 111 buys little. The 222 Sun core is mandatory and changes the rotation, so that is where a Relic selector pays off. If the cores never arrive, staying on 111 costs little: Inven's guide says "no need to go 222 from 111" and a solo-only player there clears without cores (see Caveats).

Which cores to take from the core essence pity box and the 1715 Relic Order selector: see "Your cores, and what to take from the selectors" below.

## How Time Wielder plays (Maxroll)

- **Phase Dial:** time ticks the dial forward every second, and landing skills winds it up. Each wind-up cuts every skill's remaining cooldown by 0.2s (more with Specialization) and gives the Acceleration buff (+10% attack speed, +5% cast speed, +50% MP recovery for 6s). When the minute hand passes 12 o'clock, Timeline Divergence (Z) is available.
- **Timeline Divergence** pauses the dial and your cooldowns. Every regular skill is usable once inside it, whatever its cooldown outside. Leave with Timeline Collapse (Z: damage, back to your echo), End Split Timeline (X: no damage, stay where you are), or let the 15s run out. Maxroll: forgetting Collapse loses only Collapse's own hit.
- **Your HP reverts** to what it was before Divergence when you leave. Don't drink potions inside it; use it to soak damage.
- Delayed-hit skills (Pinpoint Strike, Temporal Crush, Karmic Verdict, Shackles of Time) wind the dial when their hits land, so let them finish ticking before you press Divergence.
- Cross Thrust: charge it fully, or it hits once and winds the dial half as much. Press it twice with a half-beat gap, not as a spam: a fast second press gets eaten (Inven 3941), and without push immunity a hit also eats it (Inven 4081).
- Cooldowns freeze inside Divergence, so entering it with skills still ready loses nothing (Maxroll).

## Step 2: Time 222, once the three cores are in

**Switch when** you have **Twisted Timeline (Sun) at 17P, Timeline (Moon) at 14P, Distortion (Star) at 10P**. That is the minimum Inven says runs (업혀가나 in comments on 3941, 4089 and 378); Sun at Relic 17P is mandatory. Time 222 is the biggest loagg group (349 of 721) and has the highest ceiling: the Inven comment ranking is 222 > 112 > 111, and loagg's average combat power is about 4,410 on 222 vs about 4,130 on 111 (confounded by gear, so a hint only).

How the 222 cores change the rotation (Maxroll): Dimensional Break becomes the main damage skill and everything else exists to cut its cooldown. Twisted Timeline cuts Dimensional Break's cooldown by 5s when Destiny activates, makes casting a Fusion skill cut Cross Thrust's cooldown by 10s, and nerfs Pinpoint Strike, Temporal Crush and Boundary Break damage by 90% inside Divergence (Maxroll: Temporal Crush cast right before you exit lands in the original timeline at full damage). Timeline gives Destiny on Cross Thrust. Distortion boosts Cross Thrust.

- **Your Dimensional Annihilation** is the Time 333 Sun core. Nobody plays Time 333: 3 to 5 of 721 loagg characters, and Inven has no 333 code or priority. It gives +1.5% damage at 10P; its 14P and 17P need Destiny, which Time gets only from a Moon core you don't have. Keep it in the Order Sun slot only while you have spare gems, at 10P, until Twisted Timeline replaces it.
- **Multislash and Precise Control** are Space cores. They do nothing on Time. Register them for Core Essence.
- **Priority for the 222 cores (Inven 277, 3941, 378):** Relic: Sun > Moon > Star. Ancient: Sun = Moon > Star (277) or Sun > Moon = Star (3941).
- **111 priority, if you stay:** Relic Star > Moon > Sun; no Relic is mandatory, Legendary Sun and Moon at 14P run the whole cycle (277, 4173).

**What to take:**
1. **Core essence pity box Relic selector → Sun, Twisted Timeline.** Ark Pass astrogem → Order / DPS → auto-equip → 17P. The mandatory core goes first.
2. **1715 Rapid Growth Relic Order selector (optional honing, before 2026-12-16) → Moon, Timeline** → 17P (14P is the minimum).
3. **Star, Distortion:** 10P is enough. Equip whichever Distortion drops (Act 4 / Final Day) and register everything else.
4. **Until all three are in, play 111.** One Inven Solo player with Timeline + Distortion says one Relic ticket makes 222 possible (a commenter on 5095, 2026-10-07).
5. **Chaos:** none before 1720. At 1720 the Relic Chaos selector gives one Chaos core. Chaos cores are shared across DPS specs (Maxroll): Sun Flashy Attack (현란한 공격), Moon Smoldering Strike (불타는 일격), Star Attack (공격). loagg shows 687 / 677 / 674 of 721 on those; no source ranks them, so the choice is open.

## How the guide plays it

- Paradise plays on the base Dimensionalist identity (the Ark Passive spec doesn't apply). The Paradise gear's Specialization (800 total from Bell and Mirror) boosts the Fusion skills, awakening and clock acceleration, which is why the guide builds around the Time skills.
- The core is the dimension clock: skills with the Dial Acceleration tripod speed it up, and a faster clock cuts cooldowns, including the awakening's, which the clock passing 12 o'clock does not cut.
- Before Liquor Glass 9: on pull, ![Forewarning](assets/icons/dimensionalist/forewarning.png) Forewarning (synergy) → ![Dimensional Break](assets/icons/dimensionalist/dimensional-break.png) Dimensional Break → ![Shackles of Time](assets/icons/dimensionalist/shackles-of-time.png) Shackles of Time (awakening) → ![Karmic Verdict](assets/icons/dimensionalist/karmic-verdict.png) Karmic Verdict (hyper awakening, T) → ![Temporal Crush](assets/icons/dimensionalist/temporal-crush.png) Temporal Crush, then everything else to speed the clock. Never let awakening, hyper awakening or Dimensional Break sit off cooldown.
- After Liquor Glass 9: ![Forewarning](assets/icons/dimensionalist/forewarning.png) Forewarning → ![Karmic Verdict](assets/icons/dimensionalist/karmic-verdict.png) Karmic Verdict → ![Shackles of Time](assets/icons/dimensionalist/shackles-of-time.png) Shackles of Time → ![Temporal Crush](assets/icons/dimensionalist/temporal-crush.png) Temporal Crush → ![Dimensional Break](assets/icons/dimensionalist/dimensional-break.png) Dimensional Break → speed the clock → repeat. The rotation becomes mostly hyper awakening and awakening. Afterimage Shatter makes Temporal Crush a placed skill that fits between them, and Amass lets you spam Cross Thrust for clock speed. The author logged 7 awakening casts in one fight.
- Stuck below 60% boss HP: at 55%+ check your crit rate and retry; at 40–50% go to Elysian, run once, upgrade, and re-test before spending more entries.

## Restoration order (from the guide)

1. Mask 1 and Whistle 3 (cooldown reduction).
2. Rush Liquor Glass 3 → 6 → 9 (hyper awakening cooldown at each).
3. Bell and Mirror to 9 (cooldown reduction at 9; Specialization +200 at 3 and 6 on each, 800 total).
4. Skip the identity-meter options: Dimensionalist's identity doesn't use them.
5. After every slot is at 9: Whistle 12 (crit rate to 100%), then Censer and Mask 15 (defense ignore, crit damage on push-immune targets).

The Season 4 author's order for classes that don't use their identity agrees on the start: Mask 1 first, Liquor Glass 9 before the other level-9 steps.
