Space Wielder is the back-attack Dimensionalist build for Solo Mode. This restores the Space card that was replaced by the Time Wielder guide. The primary build is the 333 Ark Grid setup: high burst, strong utility and a piano-style rotation rather than a fixed cycle.
Codes are KR builds imported to CE; whether they play identically on global depends on global having the same Dimensionalist balance patch.

## Play this now

Space 333 with Blunt Thorn and Raid Captain. The source recommends the Order Moon → Sun → Star relic progression. The 333 build is playable with all cores at 14P, but its 17P damage gain is significant.

## How it plays

- This is a cooldown-driven piano build, not a rigid cycle. Keep the skills moving; when several are ready, use the longest cooldown first.
- The build revolves around Fate stacks, Fragment stacks and Distortion stacks. Build Fragment to six to create Distortion, then spend Distortion with ![Spatial Slash](assets/icons/dimensionalist/spatial-slash.png) Spatial Slash to refund its cooldown.
- Maintain Adrenaline with ![Forward Thrust](assets/icons/dimensionalist/forward-thrust.png) Forward Thrust and use ![Spatial Manipulation](assets/icons/dimensionalist/spatial-manipulation.png) to reach the boss's back when needed.
- The source's practical order is ![Override](assets/icons/dimensionalist/override.png) Override → ![Cross Slash](assets/icons/dimensionalist/cross-slash.png) Cross Slash → ![Vacuum](assets/icons/dimensionalist/vacuum.png) Vacuum → ![Retaliate](assets/icons/dimensionalist/retaliate.png) Retaliate, then ![Spatial Slash](assets/icons/dimensionalist/spatial-slash.png) whenever its stacks and refund are available.
- ![Spatial Slash](assets/icons/dimensionalist/spatial-slash.png) Spatial Slash's Skip tripod cancels its long startup after another skill, a normal attack, spacebar or get-up animation. Do not use the uncancelled startup when speed matters; retain it when its push immunity is useful.

## Setup

- **Engravings:** Grudge, Adrenaline, Ambush Master and Raid Captain. The fifth engraving is Mass Increase first, then Keen Blunt Weapon, Cursed Doll or another appropriate option depending on the bracelet and engraving books.
- **Stats and Ark Passive:** The checked code carries Crit 16 / Swiftness 24 and Blunt Thorn 2. Meet Raid Captain's move-speed threshold with Swiftness, pet, feast and support buffs; tune Crit through the character's efficiency table rather than copying one bracelet exactly.
- **Gems:** Prioritize damage on Spatial Slash → Override → Retaliate → Cross Slash. Prioritize cooldown on Spatial Slash → Cross Thrust → Interference, then the remaining damage skills. The source uses a 4-damage / 7-cooldown gem layout.
- **Runes:** The code carries runes on all 8 leveled skills. The source uses Judgment / Conviction for lower gem investment; with high cooldown gems, flexible Galewind and Focus assignments are viable.

## Rotation

Prepare two Distortion stacks before entering when possible. Enter with Fragment stacks active, then use Spatial Manipulation to take the back and begin the burst:

![Forward Thrust](assets/icons/dimensionalist/forward-thrust.png) Forward Thrust → ![Spatial Slash](assets/icons/dimensionalist/spatial-slash.png) Spatial Slash → ![Override](assets/icons/dimensionalist/override.png) Override → ![Cross Slash](assets/icons/dimensionalist/cross-slash.png) Cross Slash → ![Spatial Slash](assets/icons/dimensionalist/spatial-slash.png) Spatial Slash → ![Vacuum](assets/icons/dimensionalist/vacuum.png) Vacuum → ![Spatial Slash](assets/icons/dimensionalist/spatial-slash.png) Spatial Slash → ![Retaliate](assets/icons/dimensionalist/retaliate.png) Retaliate → ![Spatial Slash](assets/icons/dimensionalist/spatial-slash.png) Spatial Slash.

After the opener, use skills as they return. When multiple skills are ready, use Override → Cross Slash → Vacuum → Retaliate, keep building Fragment stacks, and spend each Distortion refund on Spatial Slash. Use Interference when its two charges are needed for push immunity, head-turn control or cooldown alignment.

## Ark Grid

The primary target is Space 333. Follow the source's Order Moon → Order Sun → Order Star progression and aim for 17P on the three cores. Until the 333 set is complete, the Space 222 runner-up is the practical alternative. Do not use Time Wielder cores on this card; the two Enlightenment trees are separate specs.

## Caveats

- The primary code was extracted verbatim from the source HTML and checked with the Skill Code Migrator: NA:yes, CE:yes, KR:yes.
- The primary source is a 2026-07-25 guide. Check tripods after importing if global's Dimensionalist balance differs from KR.
- Space Wielder is positional: use the back attack window and Spatial Manipulation rather than treating it like the non-positional Time Wielder build.
