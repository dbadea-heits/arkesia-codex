## How it plays

- Maintain Mayhem, then use ![Red Dust](assets/icons/berserker/red-dust.png) Red Dust to create the damage window.
- Fit the major damage skills inside the Red Dust buff instead of holding the whole rotation for a rigid script.
- Keep enough displayed town Crit to reach the author's target after Mayhem's enraged Crit bonus; use Swiftness nodes to keep the Sound Breakthrough speed condition active.
- The build is any-angle rather than an Ambush Master setup, but back attacks still improve the relevant skills.
- The main execution risk is fitting the long damage sequence inside Red Dust while avoiding mana loss and cooldown drift.

## Setup

- **Engravings:** Grudge, Keen Blunt Weapon, Master’s Tenacity, Raid Captain, Cursed Doll. Mana Efficiency Increase or Mass Increase can replace Cursed Doll depending on the setup; the author generally does not recommend Adrenaline.
- **Stats and Ark Passive:** Crit 18 / Swiftness 22 in the imported Evolution tree. The author uses approximately Crit 80 / Swiftness 80 on the bracelet, targets about 60% displayed town Crit, and recommends at least 22 Swiftness node points for Sound Breakthrough efficiency.
- **Gems:** Upgrade damage gems in ![Overdrive](assets/icons/berserker/overdrive.png) Overdrive → ![Finish Strike](assets/icons/berserker/finish-strike.png) Finish Strike → ![Hell Blade](assets/icons/berserker/hell-blade.png) Hell Blade → ![Sword Storm](assets/icons/berserker/sword-storm.png) Sword Storm → ![Brave Slash](assets/icons/berserker/brave-slash.png) Brave Slash → ![Mountain Crash](assets/icons/berserker/mountain-crash.png) Mountain Crash order. If upgrading cooldown gems individually, the author gives Sword Storm → Hell Blade → Finish Strike → Overdrive. Rune placement is flexible; Bleed is recommended where applicable and Focus is adjusted for mana.

## Rotation

Open with ![Red Dust](assets/icons/berserker/red-dust.png) Red Dust, then use ![Blood Slash](assets/icons/berserker/blood-slash.png) Blood Slash when available, followed by ![Overdrive](assets/icons/berserker/overdrive.png) Overdrive → ![Finish Strike](assets/icons/berserker/finish-strike.png) Finish Strike → ![Hell Blade](assets/icons/berserker/hell-blade.png) Hell Blade → ![Sword Storm](assets/icons/berserker/sword-storm.png) Sword Storm → ![Brave Slash](assets/icons/berserker/brave-slash.png) Brave Slash. Blood Slash lands approximately once every three Red Dust cycles. Use the next available skill rather than delaying the entire cycle for a perfect sequence.
