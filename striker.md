## How Deathblow plays at 1645.5

- **Core loop:** normal skills fill 4 orbs → spend all 4 on an Esoteric skill → after 16 orbs, Z → ![Charging Kick](assets/icons/battlemastermale/charging-kick.png) Charging Kick.
- **Orb rule:** ![Sky Shattering Blow](assets/icons/battlemastermale/sky-shattering-blow.png) Sky Shattering Blow and ![Vicious Tiger Dance](assets/icons/battlemastermale/vicious-tiger-dance.png) Vicious Tiger Dance give 1.5 orbs; ![Moon Flash Kick](assets/icons/battlemastermale/moon-flash-kick.png) Moon Flash Kick, ![Sweeping Kick](assets/icons/battlemastermale/sweeping-kick.png) Sweeping Kick and ![Lightning Whisper](assets/icons/battlemastermale/lightning-whisper.png) Lightning Whisper give 2.5; ![Storm Dragon Kick](assets/icons/battlemastermale/storm-dragon-kick.png) Storm Dragon Kick gives 1. A missed builder hit leaves you short of 4 orbs, and every non-crit hurts (Blunt Thorn is not recommended).
- **1640 unlocks the hyper-awakening skill**, so you already have ![Charging Kick](assets/icons/battlemastermale/charging-kick.png) Charging Kick, the 16-orb finisher.
- **One Ark Passive node picks your build:** Orb's Blessing (Enlightenment tier 4, needs 80 Enlightenment points). Check your Enlightenment points in game.
  - **With it:** the "1 hyper-awakening + Charging Kick" build (KR: 1싸1청), the pick below. Commenters say it plays identically to 111 호령 Deathblow: "same skill code, same play" (Inven 111506, 2026-07-03).
  - **Without it:** the 3-Esoteric tree with ![Advancing Strike](assets/icons/battlemastermale/advancing-strike.png) Advancing Strike, set up by hand (no code exists; see below). Switch as soon as you reach 80 Enlightenment points: at Orb's Blessing level 1 the Charging Kick build does about the same damage (Inven 95470).
- **Engravings:** Grudge, Adrenaline, Ambush Master, Super Charge + Hit Master or Precise Dagger.
- **Cards:** Light of Salvation once you use Charging Kick; Three Umar Families 3 + Fate of the Lazeniths 2 for the 3-Esoteric tree.
- **Back attacks matter:** the builds are crit-dependent, and the 333 build below requires them.

# Striker in the Paradise Crucible
