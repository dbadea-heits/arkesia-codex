## How it plays

- **Core loop:** normal skills fill 4 orbs → spend all 4 on an Esoteric skill → after 16 orbs, Z → ![Charging Kick](assets/icons/battlemastermale/charging-kick.png) Charging Kick, the 16-orb finisher (1640 unlocks the hyper-awakening skill).
- **Orb rule:** ![Sky Shattering Blow](assets/icons/battlemastermale/sky-shattering-blow.png) Sky Shattering Blow and ![Vicious Tiger Dance](assets/icons/battlemastermale/vicious-tiger-dance.png) Vicious Tiger Dance give 1.5 orbs; ![Moon Flash Kick](assets/icons/battlemastermale/moon-flash-kick.png) Moon Flash Kick, ![Sweeping Kick](assets/icons/battlemastermale/sweeping-kick.png) Sweeping Kick and ![Lightning Whisper](assets/icons/battlemastermale/lightning-whisper.png) Lightning Whisper give 2.5; ![Storm Dragon Kick](assets/icons/battlemastermale/storm-dragon-kick.png) Storm Dragon Kick gives 1. A missed builder hit leaves you short of 4 orbs.
- **Back attacks matter:** the builds are crit-dependent (every non-crit hurts; Blunt Thorn is not recommended) and the 333 build below requires them.
- **One Ark Passive node picks your build:** Orb's Blessing (Enlightenment tier 4, needs 80 Enlightenment points; check yours in game). With it, play the pick above; without it, the 3-Esoteric tree under Other builds.
- Main difficulty: orb counting under pressure, since one missed builder breaks the 4-orb cycle.

## Setup

- **Engravings:** Grudge, Adrenaline, Ambush Master, Super Charge + Hit Master or Precise Dagger
- **Stats and Ark Passive:**
  - Evolution: Crit 10 / Spec 30, Limit Break 3, Strike 2, Critical 1 + Pulverize 1, Standing Striker 2. Avoid Blunt Thorn
  - Enlightenment: everything through tier 4, plus Lethal Esoteric Skill 1 and Fully Charged 1. If you're short on points, take Fully Charged right after tier-4 level 3
  - Leap: Transcendent Power 1, Unleashed Power 5, Release Potential 3, Instant Spell 2, Efficient Strike 3

## Rotation

1. ![Storm Dragon Kick](assets/icons/battlemastermale/storm-dragon-kick.png) Storm Dragon Kick ×3 → ![Sky Shattering Blow](assets/icons/battlemastermale/sky-shattering-blow.png) Sky Shattering Blow → ![Lightning Whisper](assets/icons/battlemastermale/lightning-whisper.png) Lightning Whisper → ![Lightning Tiger Strike](assets/icons/battlemastermale/lightning-tiger-strike.png) Lightning Tiger Strike (6 Adrenaline stacks)

3. ![Sky Shattering Blow](assets/icons/battlemastermale/sky-shattering-blow.png) Sky Shattering Blow → ![Sweeping Kick](assets/icons/battlemastermale/sweeping-kick.png) Sweeping Kick → ![Lightning Tiger Strike](assets/icons/battlemastermale/lightning-tiger-strike.png) Lightning Tiger Strike
4. ![Vicious Tiger Dance](assets/icons/battlemastermale/vicious-tiger-dance.png) Vicious Tiger Dance → ![Storm Dragon Kick](assets/icons/battlemastermale/storm-dragon-kick.png) Storm Dragon Kick → ![Sky Shattering Blow](assets/icons/battlemastermale/sky-shattering-blow.png) Sky Shattering Blow → ![Call of the Wind God](assets/icons/battlemastermale/call-of-the-wind-god.png) Call of the Wind God

From the second cycle, swap the order of Sky Shattering Blow and Vicious Tiger Dance. Clock check on a Trixion dummy: run it for 2 minutes; the 6th Charging Kick lands around 1:57–1:58 (Inven 95470), and if it slips past 2:00, DPS reads low.
