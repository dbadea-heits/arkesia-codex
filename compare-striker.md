## What changes at 1640 without cores

- **The grid builds don't apply yet.** 333 Deathblow and 222 산군 Flurry need the Ark Grid cores (Inven 109943: "open the grid at 1700, get the cores, activate 14+ points").
- **1640 unlocks the hyper-awakening skill**, so Deathblow gets ![Charging Kick](assets/icons/battlemastermale/charging-kick.png) Charging Kick, its 16-orb finisher.
- **Deathblow's build depends on one Ark Passive node:** Orb's Blessing, Enlightenment tier 4, needs 80 Enlightenment points.
  - With it: the "1 hyper-awakening + Charging Kick" build (KR: 1싸1청). Commenters say it plays identically to 111 호령 Deathblow: "same skill code, same play" (Inven 111506, 2026-07-03).
  - Without it: the 3-Esoteric tree (Lightning Tiger Strike + Call of the Wind God + Tiger Emerges), with Advancing Strike as the hyper-awakening skill (Inven 95470).
  - Check your Enlightenment points in game to see which applies.
- **Flurry has fewer pre-grid options:**
  - The pre-grid build is Blunt Thorn with Call of the Wind God. Call of the Wind God was nerfed around 2026-02-11 (Inven 109872: "DPS dropped from 200M to under 150M").
  - The Supersonic Breakthrough variant doesn't work without the grid (Inven 106821).

## About the Trixion numbers (57M vs 71M)

You're probably right that Eso is stronger at 1640: the board says pre-grid Flurry is slightly ahead. But one Lightning Tiger Strike hit doesn't show it:

- **Different damage split.** In Deathblow, Lightning Tiger Strike is one of several Esoteric skills, and the 16-orb Charging Kick carries a large share of the damage. One Lightning Tiger Strike hit leaves that out.
- **Different cast frequency.** Damage per minute = hit size × casts. Flurry spends orbs differently and casts Lightning Tiger Strike on a different schedule.
- **A single hit isn't a controlled test.** Crit, back attack, Adrenaline stacks, gem levels and Trixion buffs all change it.

Fair test: run each build on the same Trixion dummy for 2 minutes and compare total DPS. The 95470 guide notes the 6th Charging Kick lands around 1:57–1:58. If it slips past 2:00, Deathblow's DPS reads low.

# Striker in the Paradise Crucible

Crucible (KR 낙원 증명) is Paradise's solo boss ladder, ranked per class. Only **skill levels and tripods** carry into Paradise: engravings, gems, runes, cards and the whole Ark Passive don't apply (official release notes; details in `paladin.md` → What applies in Paradise). So your raid spec (Deathblow / Esoteric Flurry) and Ark Grid don't matter here; only the skill bar, levels, tripods and awakening choice do.

These are KR codes, checked on [lostark.bible](https://lostark.bible/tools/skill-code) and uploaded to CE on 2026-10-15, so they import on EUC. Whether they play the same on global depends on global having KR's balance patches, which is unverified.
