# Paradise: getting the most from the weekly Hell Keys (6 characters)

Season 4: 2026-07-15 → 2027-02-03. Weekly reset Wednesday 10:00 UTC.
Every Hell reward is **character-bound**, so each character's keys pay for that character's own honing (Maxroll).

## The weekly loop (per character, ~10 min)

1. **Crucible: at least 1 attempt before the reset.** The weekly keys are a *ranking reward*. "All other participants" get 3 Rare keys, so a character that never entered the Crucible that week gets nothing at reset.
2. **Elysian**: turn on Auto-repeat and burn the 5 free entries. They drop the Legacy of Paradise gear, Restoration Stones and Crucible tickets that move your Crucible rank.
3. **Hell**: spend the keys from the reset using the run order below.

## 1. Better keys: rank higher in your class

Ranking is **per character, against the same advanced class only, region-wide** (stage → damage → clear time → Legacy of Paradise score). Weekly key rewards (official "Welcome to Paradise" notes):

| Weekly rank (in class) | Keys |
|---|---|
| Rank 1 | 2 Legendary, 3 Epic, 1 Rare |
| 2–10 | 1 Legendary, 3 Epic, 1 Rare |
| 11–30 | 3 Epic, 1 Rare |
| 31–100 | 2 Epic, 2 Rare |
| Top 10% | 2 Epic, 1 Rare |
| Top 30% | 1 Epic, 2 Rare |
| Everyone else who entered | 3 Rare |

What a better key is worth (1,000,000-run simulation, normal Hell, before Season 4's Falling Point existed):

| Key | Descents | Mean floor | Floor 100 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rare | 5 | 54 | 0% |
| Epic | 6 | 65 | 0.9% |
| Legendary | 7 | 75 | 6.7% |

Each grade is worth about **+1 reward tier**. At 1700 the gold chest goes 20k (floor 50s) → 30k (60s) → 42k (70s), so reaching **Top 30%** is worth about +10k bound gold a week on that character. That makes the Crucible worth a real attempt every week, not just a single tap to qualify.

## 2. Run order for the 3 keys

The **transmutation altar** shows up on entry (20%), and always when you land on floor 50 or 100. It lets you feed **another key from your inventory**:

- 15% −2 grades, 15% −1, 15% +1, 15% +2
- **40% → Netherworld key** (Flame or Frost, same grade)

Only **ranking keys** can be transmuted. A key that came out of the altar, or one bought with Ebony Cube tickets, can't be transmuted again.

**Netherworld is the jackpot.** 1700 gold chest by floor:

| Floor | 1–9 | 10–19 | 20–29 | 30–39 | 50–59 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Normal Hell | 4k | 6k | 8k | 10k | 20k |
| Netherworld | 20k | 30k | 40k | 50k | 100k |

A Netherworld run on the floor-1 tier pays as much as a normal run that reaches the 50s, and the realistic Netherworld result (floor ~22, see §4) is about **2×** an average normal Rare key. So the goal for the week is to have a spare key in your bag every time an altar appears.

**Order:**
1. **Enter with your best key first** (Epic/Legendary if you ranked). Keep the Rares in your bag as feed.
2. Altar appears → **feed a Rare**. Never feed the Epic or Legendary: Netherworld grade barely matters (a Legendary only reaches floor 22.5 vs 22.0 for an Epic on the safe play), so the only thing you risk is the −1/−2 grade outcome. KR consensus: "always transmute the blue key". [INFERENCE] A Rare can't drop below Rare, so it has no downside. Check the altar preview the first time.
3. Next run, use any key that **can't be fed anymore** (altar products, Netherworld keys) before your last untouched Rare. Every run you start with a feedable Rare in the bag is another altar chance.
4. Run the last untouched Rare **last**.

Three Rares give two runs with feed in the bag. The odds of turning at least one key into Netherworld are modest, about **~15% a week per character** [INFERENCE: 20% entry altar plus landing exactly on 50, × 40%], but across 6 characters that's roughly **one Netherworld key a week**.

## 3. Inside a normal Hell run

- **Hell Altars (11, 22, …, 99) are random**; you don't get to choose. Extra descent and "next descent ≥16" are the ones that move you down.
- **Falling Point** (offered when you land on a multiple of 10): 63% → 16+ floors down, 37% → climb back up.
  - **On your last descent: skip it.** From floor 60 at 1700, a normal descent is worth ~37.5k in expected gold chest. The Falling Point is worth ~36k even if a failure only drops you back into the 50s, and less if it climbs further.
  - **On earlier descents:** check whether it **used up a descent** the first time you take one (watch the counter). If it's free (an extra roll), take it every time. If it costs a descent, skip it: a normal descent averages ~10.5 floors, and 0.63 × 18 = 11.3 floors only beats that if a failure climbs back fewer than ~2 floors.
- Lucky reward (3 drops of the same distance in a row) just happens; nothing to play around.

## 4. Inside a Netherworld run

- **Flame**: landing on an **odd** floor kills you. **Frost**: landing on an **even** floor kills you (floor 100 is safe). Each descent is about a coin flip. You get **1 revive**, can stop and claim anytime, and if you die with no revive left you get **nothing**.
- **Rule: keep descending until your revive is used, then stop and claim immediately.**
  - With the revive up, descending can't lose progress.
  - Without it, one more descent is ~50% lose-everything for about one tier more (20k → 30k at 1700); it needs ~2× to break even and is never close.
  - If an altar gives an **extra revive**, you're back to "keep descending".
- The simulation (Frost, purple key): the safe play averages **floor ~22**; descending to the end averages **10** and is dead **84%** of the time.

## 5. Chest pick

At the end you choose 1 of 3 chests (4 with the Flooding altar). They're bound to the character, so pick for **that character's next hone**:

1. **The Wealth chest** (EU client name; "Abundant" in the official notes; the Abundance altar puts 10× the basic reward on one chest; at 1730 that's +52,000 shards, 260 destruction, 1,700 guardian and 40 Great Leapstones, ≈ +25k gold): almost always take it. Swap only if another chest beats it by more than ~25k, which can happen with breath on floors 50+. [INFERENCE] Unconfirmed whether the basic reward grows with floor; if it doesn't, breath wins from about floor 20.
2. **The material that character is short of** for its next step:
   - **Paladin**: use the tier list below.
   - **Dimensionalist 1700 → 1710**: per Advanced attempt it burns 1,000 blue stones, 7,000 shards, 18 leapstones and 17 fusion (`roster-plan.md`). Take whichever runs out first.
   - **Berserker (next alt to 1680)**: Advanced 1–20 and normal +10 → +14 materials.
   - **Other 1640 alts (holding)**: **gold**. Bound gold waits for their turn at 1680 without filling bag space.
3. Otherwise **Gold** on alts: always usable, never wasted. Not on the Paladin at 1700+, where gold is near the bottom (below).
4. At 80+: **Lv. 8 bound gems** (Netherworld) are worth more than any other chest for whoever uses them.

Item level brackets: Paladin and Dimensionalist use the 1700 tables (1730 tables once the Paladin is 1730); the alts use 1640 (1680 at 1680). [INFERENCE] The bracket is fixed on the key (the official 2026-02-06 note talks about "1730 keys"). **Read the key tooltip:** if it names an item level, open it now. Holding gains nothing and costs altar chances.

### Paladin chest tier list (most free hones)

How the chests are scored (2026-10-22):

- **Rewards:** Maxroll's Season 4 Hell tables (NA quantities). Chest names are LoaGuard's KR client names, translated; the NA client wording may differ slightly ("Special Honing Chest" is the name NA players use).
- **Prices:** NAE via `prices.py`.
- **Cost of one hone** on Destined Tremor +11 → +15 (cheonjang): **armor tap ≈ 11.3k gold**, **weapon tap ≈ 68.1k** (about 2,000 crystallized destruction stones at 29.5g each).
- **Breath:** full breath on Tremor is 20 per tap (loaup) and saves ~0.52 of a normal tap. So 1 bound **Lava's Breath saves ~1,770g** on the weapon (3.8× its 468g price), and 1 **Glacier's Breath saves ~295g** on armor (less than its 497g price: take it free, don't buy it).

## 6. Other key sources

- **Ebony Cube ticket → key**: if you swap, take the **Epic** key (Inven). Swapped keys **can't be transmuted**, so run them first as normal keys.
- Event Legendary Netherworld / Legendary Hell Key exchange tickets (e.g. Summer Update Gift II): spend them on the character whose honing is the bottleneck (the **Dimensionalist** until 1710), using the Netherworld rule in §4.
