# Slayer in the Paradise Crucible

Crucible (KR 낙원 증명) is Paradise's solo boss ladder, ranked per class. Only **skill levels and tripods** carry into Paradise: engravings, gems, runes, cards and the whole Ark Passive don't apply (official release notes; details in `paladin.md` → What applies in Paradise). So your raid spec (Predator) and Ark Grid don't matter here; only the skill bar, levels, tripods and awakening choice do.

These are KR codes, checked on [lostark.bible](https://lostark.bible/tools/skill-code) and uploaded to CE on 2026-10-20, so they import on EUC. Whether they play the same on global depends on global having KR's balance patches, which is unverified.
