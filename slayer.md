## How it plays

- Back-attack build: the engravings include Ambush Master, so you need to land attacks on the boss's back.
- Damage comes from the opener below, then from skills as they come off cooldown, each used from behind.
- ![Final Blow](assets/icons/berserkerfemale/final-blow.png) Final Blow and ![Furious Claw](assets/icons/berserkerfemale/furious-claw.png) Furious Claw are used back to back.
- ![Wild Stomp](assets/icons/berserkerfemale/wild-stomp.png) Wild Stomp is a party synergy skill; in solo it is just another cooldown to keep rolling.

## Setup

- **Engravings:** Grudge, Ambush Master, Mass Increase, Raid Captain, MP Efficiency Increase (thread 45138). Assumes a Crit 70 / Swiftness 70 bracelet.
- **Stats and Ark Passive:** Ark Passive order (thread 45017): Enlightenment 1 → Leap 1 → Evolution 1 → Enlightenment 5 → Leap 6 → Evolution 6 → Enlightenment 6. Leap nodes: Unleashed Power, Release Potential, Instant Spell (thread 44057).
- **Hyper awakening:** most players use ![Flame Deathblade](assets/icons/berserkerfemale/flame-deathblade.png) Flame Deathblade (thread 45110).

## Rotation

Opener (thread 45138): ![Wild Stomp](assets/icons/berserkerfemale/wild-stomp.png) Wild Stomp → ![Final Blow](assets/icons/berserkerfemale/final-blow.png) Final Blow → ![Furious Claw](assets/icons/berserkerfemale/furious-claw.png) Furious Claw → hyper awakening ![Flame Deathblade](assets/icons/berserkerfemale/flame-deathblade.png) Flame Deathblade → ![Brutal Impact](assets/icons/berserkerfemale/brutal-impact.png) Brutal Impact → ![Guillotine](assets/icons/berserkerfemale/guillotine.png) Guillotine → ![Volcanic Eruption](assets/icons/berserkerfemale/volcanic-eruption.png) Volcanic Eruption → ![Fatal Sword](assets/icons/berserkerfemale/fatal-sword.png) Fatal Sword.

## Paradise Crucible

Crucible (KR 낙원 증명) is Paradise's solo boss ladder, ranked per class. Only skill levels and tripods carry into Paradise: engravings, gems, runes, cards and the whole Ark Passive don't apply (official release notes; details in [paladin.md](paladin.md#paradise-crucible)). So your raid spec (Predator) and Ark Grid don't matter here.
