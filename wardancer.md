## How it plays

- Opens every cycle with ![Roar of Courage](assets/icons/battlemaster/roar-of-courage.png) Roar of Courage (Weakness Exposure synergy) and ![Wind's Whisper](assets/icons/battlemaster/wind-s-whisper.png) Wind's Whisper (Blessing of the Wind self-buff); ![Sweeping Kick](assets/icons/battlemaster/sweeping-kick.png) Sweeping Kick and ![Leaping Dragon](assets/icons/battlemaster/leaping-dragon.png) Leaping Dragon are the main nukes.
- **Any angle:** the pick has no Ambush Master, so it doesn't depend on attack angle.
- **Z (identity):** use it early so it automatically empowers the strongest skill of the next cycle. During the Wind's Whisper buff you can bend the cycle to put the empowerment on ![Deadly Dive](assets/icons/battlemaster/deadly-dive.png) Deadly Dive or ![Sweeping Kick](assets/icons/battlemaster/sweeping-kick.png) Sweeping Kick.
- Without the Ark Grid every skill fills only 0.2 of a Z bubble. The Order Moon core (KR nickname 초순환) later refills a full bubble on Roar of Courage / Wind's Whisper, so the author says the cycle is much harder before cores than after.
- ![Energy Combustion](assets/icons/battlemaster/energy-combustion.png) Energy Combustion is a buff you detonate on cooldown for burst damage.

## Setup

- **Engravings:** Grudge, Keen Blunt Weapon, Mass Increase, Raid Captain, Adrenaline
- **Stats and Ark Passive:** Crit 15 / Swiftness 25 Evolution with Supersonic Breakthrough 2 (see the pick's code for the full tree)
- **Gems:** Doom and Blaze level 6 on the five damage skills, Blaze on ![Wind's Whisper](assets/icons/battlemaster/wind-s-whisper.png) Wind's Whisper (gem type mapping inferred from the code). Author's gem order: ![Sweeping Kick](assets/icons/battlemaster/sweeping-kick.png) Sweeping Kick → ![Leaping Dragon](assets/icons/battlemaster/leaping-dragon.png) Leaping Dragon → ![Energy Combustion](assets/icons/battlemaster/energy-combustion.png) Energy Combustion → ![Flash Heat Fang](assets/icons/battlemaster/flash-heat-fang.png) Flash Heat Fang, highest gems first (e.g. Sweeping Kick Blaze 9 / Doom 8, Leaping Dragon Blaze / Doom 8)

## Rotation

Basic cycle: ![Roar of Courage](assets/icons/battlemaster/roar-of-courage.png) Roar of Courage → ![Wind's Whisper](assets/icons/battlemaster/wind-s-whisper.png) Wind's Whisper → ![Lightning Kick](assets/icons/battlemaster/lightning-kick.png) Lightning Kick → ![Deadly Dive](assets/icons/battlemaster/deadly-dive.png) Deadly Dive → ![Sweeping Kick](assets/icons/battlemaster/sweeping-kick.png) Sweeping Kick → ![Leaping Dragon](assets/icons/battlemaster/leaping-dragon.png) Leaping Dragon → ![Flash Heat Fang](assets/icons/battlemaster/flash-heat-fang.png) Flash Heat Fang → ![Lightning Kick](assets/icons/battlemaster/lightning-kick.png) Lightning Kick (Roar / Whisper order doesn't matter). Use Z as soon as it has 3 bubbles. Detonate ![Energy Combustion](assets/icons/battlemaster/energy-combustion.png) Energy Combustion on cooldown, ideally while the Wind's Whisper buff is up.

- **When you have to move to the boss:** Roar → Whisper → Lightning Kick → ![Sky Shattering Blow](assets/icons/battlemaster/sky-shattering-blow.png) Sky Shattering Blow → Lightning Kick → Flash Heat Fang (so nothing sits off cooldown) → move → Deadly Dive → Sweeping Kick.
- **If the boss disappears:** pre-cast Roar → Whisper, then on reappearance Lightning Kick → Deadly Dive → Sweeping Kick → Leaping Dragon → Flash Heat Fang → Lightning Kick.

## Caveats

- Supersonic Breakthrough builds feel the missing party attack-speed buff and support mana in solo (`class_compare.md` → Buffs and mechanics). If mana runs dry, take Magick Control on Flash Heat Fang.
- Both codes were checked on [lostark.bible](https://lostark.bible/tools/skill-code) and stored on CE (the pick uploaded 2026-10-20). The last KR patch to touch First Intention was 2026-04-22; the 2026-06-24 and 2026-07-22 patches only changed Esoteric Skill Enhancement and the Ark Grid cores.
- Not used, useful once you reach 1700 and own the cores: two grid-era codes with three Ark Grid cores stored, 포셔's No-Adrenaline Supersonic Breakthrough tree ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jwC_cSxvz_A), 2026-07-02) and the Blunt Thorn raid tree from an [Inven post author's comment](https://www.inven.co.kr/board/lostark/5342/162966) (2026-07-12).
