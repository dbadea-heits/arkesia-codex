# Roster guides

Reference for `roster-plan.md`: rules, costs, gold tables, Ark Grid mechanics, sources. The plan holds status, decisions and checklists only.

## Gold rules

- Each character earns gold from 3 raids/week (max 6 gold earners per roster; this roster is exactly 6). **All gold raids are Solo Mode** (Serca uses Matchmaking Mode, which you can enter alone, with Normal rewards). Extra raids beyond 3 give no gold but still give honing and raid materials.
- Resets: daily and weekly at 10:00 UTC; weekly on Wednesday.

## Ark Grid

### Rules

Ark Grid opens at 1700: the Paladin and Dimensionalist now, each alt once it passes 1700 on the way to 1710 (no event selector path for alts: drops and Core Essence only). Rules for all:
- Order cores come before Chaos. The Paladin (Base Camp) runs Chaos at 14p from the start and pushes Chaos past 14p only after Order 3×17p. The Dimensionalist (Rapid Growth) activates **no Chaos core before 1720**.
- A Legendary core tops out at 14p; 17p needs a Relic core.
- An Ancient core beats a Relic core in the same slot. The one exception is a Chaos Ancient core that isn't Tier 1, which can lose to a Relic one unless its gems are good (`paladin.md`).

### Paladin: why a support grid

Order cores only work for their own spec. Build the grid for support (Blessed Aura), since that's where the Paladin is headed. Solo Judgment doesn't need these cores: `paladin-solo.md` says not to rush the 222 core for solo.

### Dimensionalist: why 222 needs new cores

Rapid Growth character, **goal: 222**. It owns only the 3 cores in `space.md`, and none of them is a 222 core: Multislash and Precise Control are 333 cores, and Dimensional Annihilation is a Time core. 222 needs all-new cores, and **222 doesn't work on Legendary cores**: Sun and Moon must both be Relic at 17p, and Moon 17p is what makes the rotation run (`space.md`, Inven 3780). The Star core barely matters: 221 (no Star 2) did about the same damage as 222 (Inven 4508).

Relic sources: the core essence pity box (now), the Rapid Growth 1715 Order selector, the Rapid Growth 1720 Chaos selector, and Act 4 / Final Day drops. This character **needs cores**, so bonus chests on Act 4 / Final Day are worth considering.

### Astrogems

- After equipping any new core: Ark Grid menu → **auto-equip**.
- Ark Pass astrogem: choose **Order** (Paladin: SUPP, Dimensionalist: DPS) → auto-equip.
- **Process purples** with gold. Watch a cutting guide first. When the visible options are bad, reroll.
- **Fuse 2 green 8-cost + 1 blue 9/10-cost** (standard). For 3×9-cost green fusion, only when pushing Chaos cores to 14p.
- Never process greens or 9/10-cost blues. Process 8-cost blues only in a rush (burns gold).
- How to fuse: Ark Grid NPC → Astrogem Fusion. Cost = "required willpower" on the gem tooltip (8 / 9 / 10). Pick gems from the "unprocessed astrogem" list.
- Which gems to cut for a gold/damage target: https://www.loseii.com/loa-astrogem-calc/
- **Better cores come from drops, not points:** Act 4, Final Day, Serca, Horizon Cathedral (Hard drops better rarities). **Register every core you won't use** for Core Essence (Epic 1, Legendary 4, Relic 15, Ancient 50). 200 Essence = a selector chest for a Relic or Ancient core of your choice.
- More gems: Kurzan Front, Guardian Raids, 1700+ Chaos Gates, the Astrogem NPC (weekly limits for gold and for honing mats), Astrogem Shards from dismantling, Paradise. The NPC's **Uncommon–Epic Astrogem Chest takes character-bound gold**, a good sink for the Dimensionalist's bound gold once it's at 1710.

### How core points work (Maxroll Ark Grid System Guide)

- A core's points are the sum of the **yellow diamond** (points, 1–5) on its gems, up to 4 gems per core. Total gem willpower must fit the core: Epic 9, Legendary 12, Relic 15, Ancient 17. A gem's willpower = base cost (8 / 9 / 10) − its **red diamond** level, so an 8-cost gem at red 5 costs 3.
- Rarity caps the effects: Epic 10p only, Legendary 10 / 14p, Relic / Ancient 10 / 14 / 17p (+ small 18–20p bonuses).
- What it takes: Legendary 14p = 4 gems at 3 willpower (8-cost, red 5) with points 4+4+3+3. Relic 17p = 2× 8-cost + 9-cost + 10-cost, all at red 5 (3+3+4+5 = 15), with points like 5+4+4+4. **17p needs at least one gem at 5 points.**
- Processing priority: **red and yellow diamonds first**; ignore the green and blue side effects until the cores are active. Purples get 9 attempts and greens only 5, which is why only purples are worth processing. Each attempt costs 900 gold.
- Reroll whenever none of the 4 offered options raises red or yellow, or a −1 shows up on either. Always finish a gem: a bad one can still come out Relic grade (16+ total levels) and serve as fusion material.
- Fusing **processed** gems only makes sense with Relic-grade gems (re-rolls 16–18 levels across the diamonds). Fusing Legendary-grade processed gems rarely gives anything good.

### 5-point gem recipe

Purple 8-cost gem; odds from my simulation using loseii's outcome table.

1. Use a **purple 8-cost** gem (Stability / Corrosion): 9 processes, 3 rerolls (the first 2 free, the last 3,800 gold).
2. Each turn shows 4 options, and **one of them is applied at random**. Turn 1 can't be rerolled: process it.
3. From turn 2: if **none of the 4 raises points** (yellow, bottom diamond), **reroll**. Spending the paid last reroll is worth it.
4. At 5 points: if a **−1 points** option shows, reroll it away, or **Complete** if you're out of rerolls. Otherwise keep processing for red (willpower).
5. Ended below 5? Use the **Reset Ticket** (100 Blue Crystals, once per gem) and repeat.

- Odds: process every turn without thinking ≈ 23%. With steps 3–4 ≈ 39%. With a reset retry ≈ 62%. Cost ≈ 8k gold per attempt (9 × 900). A gem with 5 points **and** red 5 is ≈ 5%.
- Live help: loseii's **Advisor** tab reads a screenshot of the processing screen and says Process / Reroll / Complete: https://www.loseii.com/loa-astrogem-calc/
