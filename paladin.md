Party-raid support for the Paladin main. Supports aren't ranked on the Inven lists in [tierlist.md](tierlist.md); the Paladin's solo DPS spec is in [paladin-solo.md](paladin-solo.md).
Codes are KR builds; whether they play like KR depends on global having the same balance patches. Ark Grid: 32 Order points, up to 39 (+7).

## How it plays

- **Identity: ![Holy Aura](assets/icons/holyknight/holy-aura.png) Holy Aura (X).** Fill the Piety Meter, turn Holy Aura on, and keep it up as long as possible; it drains over 15 s (32 ticks of 3.125% every ~0.47 s). While it's on, normal skills don't build Piety, so you refill from the sources listed under Rotation.
- **Any angle.** Every buff reaches 24 m, so stand near the boss's head and take the raid's counters and stagger checks.
- Strengths: tanky; 7 s spacebar cooldown (8 s on Bard/Artist); fast motions; strong counters and burst stagger; healing costs no resource (![Holy Protection](assets/icons/holyknight/holy-protection.png) Holy Protection plus the Holy Aura heal).
- Weaknesses: hard to recover after something goes wrong (no burst heal); limited buff ceiling (Piety locks during Holy Aura, ![Heavenly Blessings](assets/icons/holyknight/heavenly-blessings.png) Heavenly Blessings has a 36 s cooldown); no attack-reduction debuff on the boss.
- Main difficulty is buff uptime: never let the attack buff drop. Hitting the boss for Piety matters less than that.

## Rotation

- **Before the raid:** fill Piety and 10 Magick Stream stacks at the repair NPC, and trigger Destiny.
- **Opener:** ![Heavenly Blessings](assets/icons/holyknight/heavenly-blessings.png) Heavenly Blessings → spacebar → ![Holy Aura](assets/icons/holyknight/holy-aura.png) Holy Aura (X) → ![Divine Justice](assets/icons/holyknight/divine-justice.png) Divine Justice (T) → ![Divine Wave](assets/icons/holyknight/divine-wave.png) Divine Wave (Z) → brand skill → ![Holy Sword](assets/icons/holyknight/holy-sword.png) Holy Sword. Holy Sword goes last because casting it too early wastes Piety past the cap.
- **Loop:** awakening → Holy Aura → rebuild Piety → … Turn Holy Aura on whenever Piety is full, unless a burst window is coming soon. Stay close and hit the boss; standing far away wastes Piety gain.
- **Piety refills during Holy Aura:** awakening potion, awakening (80%), Heavenly Blessings / ![Wrath of God](assets/icons/holyknight/wrath-of-god.png) Wrath of God on hit, Divine Wave (Z), T skill, and Holy Sword with Sword's Prayer.
- **Piety builder:** ![Light of Judgment](assets/icons/holyknight/light-of-judgment.png) Light of Judgment (no Piety refill during Holy Aura; 2 levels of destruction).
- **Attack buffs:** Heavenly Blessings (damage reduction, mana regen, attack speed, Piety refill on hit; longer cooldown, press it on cooldown) and Wrath of God (push immunity, Piety refill on hit; use it to fill gaps).
- **Shields:** ![God's Decree](assets/icons/holyknight/god-s-decree.png) God's Decree (shield plus emergency save, push immunity), ![Holy Protection](assets/icons/holyknight/holy-protection.png) Holy Protection (shield plus small heal, no push immunity, easiest to drop), Divine Wave Z (small shield, refills Piety; use it off cooldown even if shields overlap). Don't overlap Decree and Protection unless it's a big hit and awakening is down. Holy Protection tripod: Lingering Power or Robust Protection; Purify only for raids that need it.
- **Counter / stagger:** ![Executioner's Sword](assets/icons/holyknight/executioner-s-sword.png) Executioner's Sword (fast counter, destruction, stagger; push immunity with Sword's Prayer; save it for mechanics) and Holy Sword (with Release Light it counters from long range up to 3 times; with Sword's Prayer it has 2 charges and refills Piety, so keep using it).
- **Awakening:** refills 80% Piety as you jump (even during Holy Aura) and gives the party a 100% max-HP shield for 10 s. Hit the boss when Holy Aura has just ended; its hit also builds Piety. Don't save it unless a pattern specifically needs it.
- **Hyper awakening (T, Divine Justice):** about 30 s after cooldown reduction; +10% damage for 10 s and +20% hyper awakening damage for 20 s. Line it up with Holy Aura when possible, but otherwise use it whenever you can.

## Ark Grid

Core names are global's, from [Maxroll's Ark Grid overview](https://maxroll.gg/lost-ark/resources/ark-grid-overview). The Star core changes how you play; Sun and Moon cores only add buff strength. The Sun core's 14P effect triggers on Destiny, and only a Moon core at 14P+ activates Destiny, so **the Moon core needs 14P for the Sun core's main effect to work**.

| Slot | Core | 10P | 14P | 17P |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sun 1 | Heavenly Agent | Ally Atk +1.3% | Destiny → +5% stagger dmg | Ally Atk +5.6/8.5% |
| Sun 2 | **True Justice** | Ally Atk +1.3% | Destiny → +7% shields/heals on party | Divine Justice (T skill) ally damage buff +27/40% |
| Sun 3 | Blessing of Light | Ally Atk +1.3% | Destiny → self −5% damage taken | Holy Aura ally damage buff +6.5/10% |
| Moon 1 | Heavenly Resolve | Ally dmg +1.5% | Heavenly Blessings triggers Destiny | Ally Atk +5.6/8.5% |
| Moon 2 | **Divine Cause** | Ally dmg +1.5% | Holy Protection triggers Destiny | Divine Justice ally damage buff +27/40% |
| Moon 3 | Divine War | Ally dmg +1.5% | Divine Wave (Z) triggers Destiny | Holy Aura ally damage buff +6.5/10% |
| Star 1 | **Sword's Prayer** | Executioner's Sword + Holy Sword 20% faster cast, −70% mana | Executioner's Sword gets push immunity; Holy Sword applies a 5 s brand | Holy Sword with Release Light: fixed 6/9% Piety gain (also during Holy Aura), **2 charges** |
| Star 2 | Prayer of Divine Surge | Holy Explosion 20% faster cast | Prepared Explosion: 2 charges | Final blast refills 2/3% Piety |
| Star 3 | Prayer of Light | Light of Judgment stagger → Mid-High | Light of Judgment 20% faster cast | Light of Judgment Piety gain +32/48% |

18–20P on every core add a small bonus of the same type.

**Target (Tier-1 setup, 17P Relic), from the certified guide:** Order Sun True Justice, Order Moon Divine Cause, Order Star Sword's Prayer; Chaos Sun Faith Enhancement, Chaos Moon Echoing Brand, Chaos Star Weapon. Maxroll's global guide recommends Echoing Steel instead of Echoing Brand for the Chaos Moon; the rest matches.

**Selection:**

- An Ancient core beats a Tier-1 Relic core, so equip it in that slot. Exception: a non-Tier-1 Chaos Ancient core with careless gems can give less Combat Power than a Relic one; finish the slots you have no Relic core in first.
- Crafting order for Relic cores (Order Star excluded; craft it first if you want its utility): Order Moon ≥ Order Sun > Chaos Moon > Chaos Sun > Chaos Star. The Relic Sun buffs very slightly more than the Moon, but the Moon saves gem cost on triggering Destiny.
- Every code above, the Holy Explosion one included, assumes Star Sword's Prayer; the simple guide calls it fixed. Prayer of Divine Surge (2 Holy Explosion charges) is the only Star core that helps a brand skill, but neither guide recommends it.

**Sun / Moon pair by buff uptime** ([post 89197](https://www.inven.co.kr/board/lostark/5558/89197), 2026-10-02): both guides name True Justice / Divine Cause as the best pair, but a later post says each pair strengthens one buff, so pick the buff your party keeps up. The core-to-buff mapping is inferred from the core effects above. The KR nicknames are the post's.

| KR nickname | Pair | Buff it strengthens | Pick it when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 아리아 ("aria", the T-skill buff) | True Justice + Divine Cause | ![Divine Justice](assets/icons/holyknight/divine-justice.png) Divine Justice | You time T with your DPS (static / Discord). The author's pug uptime is only about 40–50%: short buff, and players often fire it early for Piety |

**Spending 32 (+7) points** (my reading of the core text; neither guide gives a split):

1. Star Sword's Prayer → **17P**. All the recommended codes assume it (2 ![Holy Sword](assets/icons/holyknight/holy-sword.png) Holy Sword charges and Piety gain during Holy Aura). At 14P you only get the Holy Sword brand.
2. Moon → **14P**, so Destiny triggers and the Sun core's 14P effect works.
3. That is 31 of 32 points. Spend the +7 on raising Moon or Sun toward 17P, which adds the large Divine Justice buff with True Justice / Divine Cause.

## Paradise Crucible

KR Paradise Season 4 started 2026-09-09; global's Season 4 runs 2026-07-15 → 2027-02-03, so the stages may not match. Crucible ranks you against every Paladin and ignores your spec, so both codes here are the Paladin's pick for any build. Whether they play the same on global depends on global having KR's 2026-06-24 Paladin balance patch, which is unverified. Content: Paradise **Crucible** (KR 낙원 증명), solo boss stages ranked by stage, then damage, then clear time ([paradise.md](paradise.md)).

**What applies in Paradise.** From the official [Welcome to Paradise release notes](https://www.playlostark.com/en-us/game/releases/welcome-to-paradise): "Only skill levels and skill traits are retained." These do **not** apply:

- Character gear, accessories, bracelet and ability stones
- **Engravings**
- Cards (equipped sets and collection effects)
- **Gems**
- **Ark Passive**: Evolution, Enlightenment and Elixirs, including the Evolution stat points
- Alchemy, Transcendence
- Collectible stat bonuses
- **Skill runes**
- Pet effects

Also, every attack counts as a head and back attack, and attacks on higher-grade monsters get Weak Point automatically (from global's first Paradise season, July 2025; Maxroll's guide, updated for Season 4 on 2026-07-23, still says Paradise ignores your normal gear and systems). So in a Crucible code only the skill bar, skill levels, tripods, awakening and hyper awakening choice matter; the engravings, gems, runes and Ark Passive stored in it do nothing. Crit comes only from the Paradise gear (Whistle 9 Crit Rate, Mask Crit Damage).

Gear slots: Censer, Whistle, Mask, Liquor Glass, Bell, Mirror (Legacy of Paradise); Restoration raises a slot's level, Resonance upgrades its item skill.

### Restoration order

Each number is the Restoration level to bring that gear slot to. Go left to right; slots joined by `/` all go to that level. The order is the author's for characters that use their identity gauge. Effects come from the [Maxroll Paradise guide](https://maxroll.gg/lost-ark/resources/paradise-guide) (global's table; KR Season 4 may differ).

- **Phase 1 (levels 1–4, week 1):** Mask 1 → Censer 1 → Liquor Glass / Bell / Mirror 2 → Mask / Censer / Whistle 2 → Whistle 3 → Liquor Glass 3 → Whistle 4 → Bell / Mirror 3 → Censer 4 → the rest to 4. The author says the order barely matters here: clearing Crucible 1–2 and farming Elysian 3 puts every slot at 4 anyway. He gives no level for "the rest" after the level-2 step; 2 is my reading
- **Phase 2 (level 6):** Censer 6 → Liquor Glass 6 → Whistle 6 → Mask 6, then Bell / Mirror. Whistle 6 makes the identity gauge fill much faster; the author says any order clears the content at this point
- **Phase 3 (level 9):** **Liquor Glass 9 first** (hyper awakening cooldown), then Censer 9 (ignore defense on bosses), then everything else to 9. From Crucible 6 on, a character without Liquor Glass 9 falls behind badly; by about week 9–10, characters not at 9 on every slot struggle
- **Resonance:** once a slot reaches 9 with a Legendary item in it, its item skill unlocks and Resonance upgrades it (Maxroll). The author: always do it
