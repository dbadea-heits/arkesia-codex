## How it plays

- Damage comes in **Burst** (Z): enter it, then empty the Burst order below, with ![Bloody Rush](assets/icons/berserker/bloody-rush.png) Bloody Rush as the identity finisher.
- Pre-grid it's a **Finish build**: ![Finish Strike](assets/icons/berserker/finish-strike.png) Finish Strike is the centrepiece.
- **Any angle**: no Ambush Master in any build here.
- Harder than Mayhem before Ark Grid cores; one Dec 2025 Inven post advises Mayhem until 1700 for that reason.

## Setup

- **Engravings:** Grudge, Raid Captain, Keen Blunt Weapon, Cursed Doll, Mass Increase
- **Stats and Ark Passive:** Crit + Specialization bracelet, Critical elixir; Evolution Specialization 30 / Crit 10 with Strike 2; Stampede in Enlightenment
- **Gems:** damage gems on ![Bloody Rush](assets/icons/berserker/bloody-rush.png) Bloody Rush first; at 1700+ also on ![Power Break](assets/icons/berserker/power-break.png) Power Break

## Rotation

Right after entering Burst (Z): ![Overdrive](assets/icons/berserker/overdrive.png) Overdrive → ![Tempest Slash](assets/icons/berserker/tempest-slash.png) Tempest Slash → ![Whirlwind](assets/icons/berserker/whirlwind.png) Whirlwind → ![Red Dust](assets/icons/berserker/red-dust.png) Red Dust, then ![Finish Strike](assets/icons/berserker/finish-strike.png) Finish Strike and ![Bloody Rush](assets/icons/berserker/bloody-rush.png) Bloody Rush.
