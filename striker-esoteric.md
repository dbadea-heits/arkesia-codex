## How it plays

- **Identity loop:** use two normal skills to build orbs, then spend them on an Esoteric skill; repeat instead of waiting for a full four-orb Deathblow burst.
- **Back attacks matter:** this remains a positional build, so Rear Ambush and the crit threshold depend on consistent back access.
- **Speed first:** Swiftness 30 makes the rotation quick and reduces downtime, but low-level cooldown gems can still make the loop uneven.
- **Main risk:** the base tree has no super-armor comfort skill. Use the runner-up below if interruptions are more frustrating than lost damage.

## Setup

- **Engravings:** Grudge, Adrenaline, Raid Captain, Ambush Master, Mass Increase. Keen Blunt Weapon can replace Mass Increase where the character's crit setup supports it.
- **Stats and Ark Passive:** Evolution Crit 10 / Swiftness 30 with Keen Sense 1 and Optimized Training 2. Enlightenment: Esoteric Flurry 1, Untouchable 3, Esoteric Skill Focus 3, Rear Ambush 1, Flurry Enhancement 2, Taijutsu Enhancement 5.
- **Gems:** prioritize a damage gem on ![Charging Kick](assets/icons/battlemastermale/charging-kick.png) Charging Kick; then ![Sweeping Kick](assets/icons/battlemastermale/sweeping-kick.png) Sweeping Kick, ![Vicious Tiger Dance](assets/icons/battlemastermale/vicious-tiger-dance.png) Vicious Tiger Dance and the remaining Esoteric skills. Cooldown gems matter on the normal builders.
- **Cards:** use the character's available crit/back-attack card setup; the source's Ark Grid card context does not prove a separate Solo recommendation.
